Selected work

Two systems, built end to end.

Haika designed, built and deployed both of these as demonstrations. Everything below describes what each build actually does — no client-reported results are claimed for either.

01

Amrita Dental

SmileCare Dental & Maxillofacial Center · Hyderabad

  • Website
  • AI chat
  • Staff system
  • Demo
  1. Client
    Amrita Dental — SmileCare Dental & Maxillofacial Center, a dental practice in Hyderabad.
  2. Problem
    Two audiences, two different needs: a patient deciding whether to trust the practice before ever calling, and staff keeping a day’s appointments straight without a shared view of it.
  3. What Haika built
    A public website with appointment requests, WhatsApp contact and an embedded AI assistant — plus a separate staff admin with an appointment dashboard, status tracking and a rolling week view.
  4. System
    • Public website
    • AI chat
    • Staff admin
    • Appointment records
  5. Interaction / demo
    Open the public siteamrita-s-dentist.vercel.app Open the staff systemadmin-dentist-portal.vercel.app
  6. Result / status
    Live demo. Both surfaces are deployed and explorable. No performance figures are attached to it.

The system

Presence, conversation, automation — drawn as one assembly.

Haika draws the whole thing before building any of it: the surface people arrive on, the layer that answers them, and the machinery that runs underneath. All three land on the same structure.

  1. 01 — Presence

    The layer people arrive on.

    A site or interface built to carry the weight of the business: considered, fast, and unmistakably its own.

    • Website
    • Landing pages
    • Brand surface
  2. 02 — Conversation

    The layer that answers back.

    Chat and voice that respond in the business’s own voice, guide the visitor and pick up the repetitive questions.

    • Chatbot
    • Voice
    • Qualification
  3. 03 — Automation

    The layer that keeps moving.

    Databases, workflows and the tools already in use — wired together so information moves and actions fire on their own.

    • Workflows
    • Records
    • Integrations
